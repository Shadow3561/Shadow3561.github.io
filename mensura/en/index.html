Measure your home in minutes – no tape measure.

Mensura scans every room with your iPhone’s LiDAR sensor. You get a floor plan, areas and material quantities – for renovating at home just as much as for a trade business.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad

How much paint, flooring or tile do I need?

When you renovate you’re often guessing: how big is the room really, how much wall is left after doors and windows, how many square metres of flooring should I buy? Mensura answers all of that after a single walk around the room – with waste allowance already included.

  • Wall area
  • Floor area
  • Ceiling area
  • Skirting
  • Doors & windows deducted

How it works

Scan

Move your iPhone around the room. Walls, doors, windows and furniture are detected.

Check

Delete or move anything detected wrongly, adjust dimensions by hand if needed.

Get results

Floor plan, areas and material quantities are ready right away.

Share

Send as PDF or spreadsheet to family, the DIY store or a contractor.

Better quotes – with accurate measurements

Contractors can only quote as precisely as the measurements you give them. With a dimensioned floor plan as a PDF you can request quotes precisely, compare them on the same basis and avoid surcharges caused by badly estimated areas.

Also handy: will the sofa fit? How big is the flat really? How much space do you have when moving or renting?

From survey to finished quote

Professionals enter their prices once and turn a survey straight into a quote – including material, fittings and cables. Exports as PDF, CSV (Excel), DXF (CAD) and 3D model are included.

Your data stays with you

Mensura needs no account and sends no project data to us. There are no ads and no tracking. Optionally you can back up your data to a folder of your choice, for example iCloud Drive.

All measurements and calculations are approximations from the LiDAR scan and do not replace an on-site check or calculation by a qualified planner or electrician. The images on this page are illustrations with sample values.
