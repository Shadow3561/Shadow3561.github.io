2. The Rastivo app

Rastivo stores projects, rooms, dimensions, photos, notes, company details, prices and settings exclusively on your device. We do not receive any of this data. The app needs no user account and contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no third-party SDKs that transmit data.

The app uses the camera and the LiDAR sensor to measure rooms and to take photos for notes. Processing takes place on the device. Nothing is transmitted.

Settings (e.g. deduction rules, company details, price list) are stored locally in device storage (UserDefaults). The company logo and photos are kept in the app’s storage.

3. Backup to a folder of your choice

In the settings you can choose a folder in the Files app to which the app copies your projects, photos, company logo and settings as files. If the folder is with a cloud provider (e.g. iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox), the files are transferred by that provider’s app and its privacy terms apply. We have no access to them.

4. Export and sharing

PDF, CSV, DXF and 3D files are generated locally. When you share a file, this happens through the iOS Share function. You decide where the file goes; the recipient processes it under its own rules.

5. Purchase

The app is purchased through the Apple App Store. Apple processes order and payment data as an independent controller under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We receive no payment data.

6. Visiting this website and hosting by GitHub Pages

This static website is served by GitHub Pages. According to GitHub, visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security reasons when a GitHub Pages site is accessed, and other technically necessary connection data may be processed.

The purpose is secure delivery of the website; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation). More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

The Rastivo pages set no cookies, embed no analytics or advertising services, external fonts or videos, and store nothing in your browser.

7. Contact by email

If you email us, we process your sender address and the content of your message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed and no statutory retention duty applies.

8. Your rights

Within the legal requirements you have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To exercise your rights, a message to enzym.plane.7q@icloud.com is enough.

9. Changes

We update this policy when the app, the website, the services used or legal requirements change. Status: November 2026.
