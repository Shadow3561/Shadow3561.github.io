Frequently asked questions

My iPhone has no LiDAR sensor. Can I use Mensura?

Yes. Scanning needs LiDAR (iPhone Pro models from 12 Pro and iPad Pro with LiDAR). You can also create rooms by hand with length, width and height.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. You can back it up to a folder of your choice and restore it from there.

How do I delete my data?

Projects can be deleted in the app. Deleting the app also removes the locally stored data. A backup lives in the folder you chose, and you delete it there yourself.

Are the calculations binding?

No. Dimensions, heat load, material and cable lengths are guideline values and must be verified on site.
