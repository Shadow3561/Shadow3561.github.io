Room measurement, materials & quotes

Mensura scans rooms with your iPhone’s LiDAR sensor and turns them into floor plans, areas, material lists and quotes – for painters, floor layers, tilers, plumbing/heating and electricians.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad

What Mensura does

Scan or type

Capture rooms with LiDAR or enter dimensions by hand. Walls, doors, windows and stairs are detected and can be corrected.

Floor plan and dimensions

Dimensioned floor plan with wall, door and window numbers, sloped ceilings, deduction rules and waste allowance.

For your trade

Painters, floor layers, tilers, plumbing/heating and electricians: scope per room, tile height, baseboards and more.

Heat load and routing

Simplified heat load per DIN EN 12831, radiator suggestions, plus pipe and cable lengths from connection points in the floor plan.

Materials and quotes

Material list with fittings and cables and a PDF quote from your own price list.

Export

PDF, CSV (Excel), DXF (CAD) and 3D model as USDZ or SCN. With your company header.

Your data stays with you

Mensura needs no account and sends no project data to us. There is no advertising and no tracking. Optionally, back up your data to a folder of your choice, for example in iCloud Drive.

All dimensions and calculations are estimates from the LiDAR scan. They do not replace an on-site check or calculations by a qualified planner or electrician.
